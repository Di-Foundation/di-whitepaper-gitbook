AI Quant Intelligence is DI's product extension for quantitative research, market structure analysis, and risk factor research.

It is built on Event Engine, real compute capacity, the 800TB crypto-native model, and Agent Network. It helps users turn events, on-chain data, market structure, fund flows, sentiment narratives, and historical samples into researchable, backtestable, and monitorable quantitative variables and risk factors.

AI Quant Intelligence improves research efficiency, risk identification, and market structure analysis inside DI's intelligence ecosystem.

## Product Positioning

AI Quant Intelligence is positioned as a research and market structure analysis tool. It supports event factor generation, historical sample analysis, risk exposure identification, market structure monitoring, and research report generation.

Its core position is to extend DI's event intelligence, data capability, model capability, and compute capability into quantitative research and market structure analysis.

Users can use AI Quant Intelligence to analyze whether certain event types have affected specific assets, whether on-chain behaviors correlate with market volatility, whether market structure variables indicate rising risk, or what risk exposure may appear under a scenario.

Its core value is helping users understand variables, risks, and market structure more systematically.

## Relationship With Event Engine

AI Quant Intelligence and Event Engine share the same compute, data, model, and Agent foundation, but serve different use cases.

Event Engine focuses on event understanding and scenario simulation, helping users answer how an event may affect the market. AI Quant Intelligence focuses on quantitative research and market structure analysis, helping users turn events, on-chain behavior, fund flows, and historical samples into researchable, backtestable, and monitorable variables.

They are connected but not subordinate. Event Engine provides the event intelligence entrance. AI Quant Intelligence provides the research and analysis entrance.

## Core Capabilities

AI Quant Intelligence supports multiple research capabilities.

Event factor generation: the system can turn event simulation results into quantitative research variables, such as event intensity, event propagation speed, abnormal on-chain fund level, exchange liquidity pressure, stablecoin risk index, derivatives leverage risk, sentiment heat change, macro shock intensity, and asset correlation change.

Historical event backtesting: users can backtest event categories based on the historical event database, such as the impact of regulatory events across sectors, the effect of stablecoin depeg events on market liquidity, the effect of on-chain security incidents on protocols and ecosystems, and the impact of macro rate changes on risk assets.

Risk exposure analysis: the system can help users analyze potential risk exposure for an asset, portfolio, or research object under different event scenarios.

Market structure monitoring: the system can monitor liquidity depth, funding rates, open interest, liquidation data, exchange net inflows and outflows, stablecoin supply and flows, volatility, order book changes, asset correlations, and on-chain fund migration.

Research report generation: the system can integrate event simulations, quantitative variables, backtest results, risk analysis, and market structure monitoring into structured research reports.

## Use Cases

AI Quant Intelligence can be used for event-driven research, asset risk analysis, on-chain fund behavior research, stablecoin risk monitoring, exchange risk monitoring, macro and Crypto correlation research, strategy research assistance, scenario stress testing, institutional research reports, developer data tools, and enterprise risk systems.
