$Di allocation will be built around long-term ecosystem construction, product growth, real contributions, long-term team input, partner collaboration, and ecosystem launch needs.

DI will keep allocation mechanisms long-term oriented so resources serve product development, compute input, model iteration, data construction, developer ecosystem growth, contribution incentives, and long-term governance.

## Allocation Directions

Ecosystem and product incentives will support user growth, product usage, developer ecosystem growth, Compute Packs, API services, Agent ecosystem, enterprise collaboration, and community building.

Data, model, and calibration contribution rewards will incentivize high-quality data, model optimization, Agent building, historical review, risk identification, security feedback, and developer tools.

The Foundation long-term treasury will support compute input, model R&D, data procurement, ecosystem funds, security construction, market expansion, partners, and long-term reserves.

Team and long-term contributor allocation will incentivize the core team, technical contributors, product contributors, research contributors, and long-term builders.

Strategic partner and ecosystem ally allocation will support long-term ecosystem collaboration, resource coordination, channel development, technical cooperation, and strategic building.

Ecosystem launch and service access reserves will support early user service quotas, developer access, partner pilots, API and Compute Packs launch, and ecosystem task budgets.

## Release Principles

$Di release will follow long-term, staged, contribution-oriented, and governance-transparent principles.

Ecosystem and product incentives will be released in stages based on real product progress, user growth, developer building, and ecosystem tasks.

Data, model, and calibration contribution rewards will be released in stages based on contribution quality, review performance, model improvement, Agent stability, and ecosystem value.

The Foundation long-term treasury will use long-term budget management to support DI's continuous construction.

Team and long-term contributors, strategic partners, and related ecosystem allocations will use long-term vesting, staged unlocking, and transparent disclosure arrangements to align with DI's long-term development.

Ecosystem launch and service access reserves will support early service access, developer task launch, partner pilots, and ecosystem application bootstrapping.

## Governance and Disclosure

DI will disclose more complete allocation and release details in later stages, including total supply, allocation percentages, release cadence, vesting arrangements, treasury management, and governance processes.

At the current stage, DI uses the basic principles and directions of $Di allocation as the basis for public communication and keeps allocation design aligned with real products, real contributions, and long-term ecosystem construction.
