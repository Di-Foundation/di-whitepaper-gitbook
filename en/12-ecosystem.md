# 12. Ecosystem & Computing Support

DI’s products depend on data, research, strategies, models and computing resources. Ecosystem collaboration allows outside teams to contribute specific capabilities without requiring every participant to take the same role.

## Research and product collaboration

Research and IP partners, external quant strategies and product teams can add capabilities to DI’s models and products. Their value should appear in usable research, tools or products, connected through clear scope and rules.

The Quant Platform provides one concrete entry point. Creators can turn strategies into products that other users can understand and use. The later Agent network will further connect capabilities supplied by different participants.

## Data and model contributions

Verified data and other material valuable to the models and products are the focus of contribution rewards. Ecosystem co-development supports research/IP, external strategies and product collaboration. Both support the ecosystem but serve different purposes.

Contribution value cannot be assessed only by submission volume. Relevance, source quality and practical use affect whether content can support products and user tasks.

## The role of computing resources

Computing resources support data processing, model calls, multi-perspective research and strategy analysis. The team’s experience operating computing centers in China and Southeast Asia provides relevant operational background.

Deliverable capabilities still depend on specific functionality, usage conditions and service performance. Access to compute does not establish that a product is available, nor does it prove analytical accuracy or strategy returns.

## Extension directions

Compute service quotas, APIs and enterprise services can provide additional ways for developers and teams to access capabilities. These directions need to develop around actual product functions and service conditions.

The core products in this edition remain the Data Dashboard, Event Engine and Quant Platform. Extension directions are not presented as a list of launched standalone products and do not replace the path toward Personal Agents and the network.
