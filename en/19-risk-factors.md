DI's construction involves AI, compute, data, crypto markets, Web3 coordination, and global operations. Relevant risks need to be continuously identified, managed, and updated.

## Technical Risk

AI models, Agent workflows, data processing, compute scheduling, and API services may experience errors, latency, bias, or unavailability.

DI will reduce technical risk through model evaluation, system monitoring, rollback mechanisms, data validation, Agent reviews, and security checks.

## Data Risk

Crypto market data sources are complex. Data from different platforms may have delays, missing values, errors, duplication, manipulation, or interpretation bias.

DI will improve data reliability through multi-source cross-validation, data quality scoring, anomaly detection, manual review, and contributor reputation mechanisms.

## Model Risk

AI models may hallucinate, overfit, misunderstand context, amplify bias, or respond inadequately to extreme events.

DI will continuously optimize model performance through historical reviews, model calibration, Agent cross-validation, multi-scenario outputs, and uncertainty labeling.

## Market Risk

Crypto markets are volatile. Extreme market conditions, declining liquidity, regulatory changes, security incidents, stablecoin risk, exchange risk, and macro shocks may affect user behavior and ecosystem development.

DI will continue expanding risk identification capabilities and advance system development around real product usage and long-term ecosystem construction.

## Compliance Risk

Different jurisdictions have different requirements for digital assets, AI services, data services, token mechanisms, privacy protection, and user protection.

DI will continue monitoring applicable rules and adjust product, disclosure, governance, and operational arrangements based on business development and market entry paths.

## Operational Risk

Compute costs, model training costs, data procurement costs, security spending, ecosystem incentives, partner execution, and market expansion may affect DI's long-term operations.

DI will improve operational resilience through staged construction, budget management, treasury governance, partner collaboration, and resource diversification.
